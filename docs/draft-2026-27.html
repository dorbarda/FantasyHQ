Who Took Who

Ordered by draft slot. Each row: round, player, projected avg FPG, value vs. ESPN rank. Rounds 11–13 are shown faded and are not counted in the stats.

Source. ESPN Fantasy league data and ESPN's 2026-27 preseason projections under our league's points scoring, pulled 18 Oct 2026, the day after the draft. Projections change daily, so these numbers are a snapshot.

How it is counted. Proj. points = sum of each player's projected season total. Avg FPG = simple average of the ten players' projected points per game. Proj. GP = sum of projected games played. Value = sum of (pick number − ESPN rank); positive means the team got players later than ESPN ranked them. vs. ADP = the same idea against ESPN's average draft position across all leagues.
